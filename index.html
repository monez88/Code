🇳🇿 About Daily Word NZ

A simple website designed to help people build their English vocabulary one word at a time.

📖 Our Purpose

Daily Word NZ helps students and English learners discover useful new words every day. Each word includes a definition, example sentence, and a challenge to help you remember it.

🎯 Our Goal

Our goal is to make learning vocabulary simple, enjoyable, and accessible. You don't need to spend hours studying. Learning just one word each day can help build your vocabulary over time.

💡 How It Works

Visit the Home page to see today's word. Explore the Words page to discover more vocabulary, then test yourself with the Daily Challenge.
